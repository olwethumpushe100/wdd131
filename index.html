    Why hike with TrailMate

    Safer in a group

    Mountain weather changes quickly. Hiking with others means help is always close by.

    Local knowledge

    Our hike leaders know the trails, the best times to start and where the views are.

    A friendly pace

    Every hike has a level, so you can choose one that matches your fitness and experience.
