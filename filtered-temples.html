About This Album

This page brings together temples from around the world. Use the menu to filter the album by age or size, or choose Home to see every temple again. See the full list of temples.

How the Filters Work

Old shows temples dedicated before 1900 and New shows those dedicated after 2000. Large shows temples over 90,000 square feet and Small shows temples under 10,000 square feet. Learn more about me on my home page.
