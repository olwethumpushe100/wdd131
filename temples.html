About This Album

This page brings together temples from around the world, including several across the African continent close to home. It's a small, growing gallery I'll keep adding to as I visit or learn about more. See the full list of temples.

Why These Temples

I chose to feature the Salt Lake Temple alongside the temples serving South Africa, Ghana, Nigeria, the DR Congo, and Zimbabwe — a mix of the historic and the ones nearest to where I live. Learn more about me on my home page.
