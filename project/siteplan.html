Site Name

TrailMate Hiking Club

The name is short and memorable. "Trail" tells visitors the site is about hiking, and "Mate" signals a friendly community where members find hiking partners.

Optional domain availability: trailmate-hiking.org

Site Purpose

The site is a hub for local hikers. It provides:

  • Trail recommendations with difficulty, distance and photos.
  • Gear advice for beginners and experienced hikers.
  • An events calendar of group hikes.
  • A membership information form for new members.

Scenarios

Questions a visitor from the target audience might ask:

  1. What is a good beginner trail I can finish in half a day?
  2. What gear do I need for a day hike in cold weather?
  3. When is the next group hike, and how do I sign up?

Color Schema

These colors are used on this document and will be used on the final site.

  • Forest Green #1f4d3a Header and footer backgrounds, headings, and navigation.
  • Trail Rust #b34712 Links, buttons, borders and other accents.
  • Mist #f4f7f1 Page background and text on dark backgrounds.
  • Charcoal #1c1c1c Paragraph and body text.

Typography

  • Montserrat (600 and 700)

    Used for the site name, headings and navigation links.

  • Source Sans 3 (400 and 600)

    Used for paragraphs, lists, forms and captions.

Both fonts come from Google Fonts. Fallbacks are sans-serif.
